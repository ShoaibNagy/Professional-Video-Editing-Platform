How a project moves through VideoPM

Six phases, the same order, every time — with a checklist gate between each one.

  1. 1

    Ingest & Organize

    Footage lands in the standard folder structure and matching software bins.

  2. 2

    Edit Prep

    Clips get rated Reject, Maybe, or Select before the creative edit starts.

  3. 3

    Rough Cut & Edit

    The story gets built, from rough cut to fine cut.

  4. 4

    Color & Audio QC

    A checklist gates this phase: white balance, levels, track separation.

  5. 5

    Export & Delivery

    Platform presets render the final files, checked once more before delivery.

  6. 6

    Review & Improve

    What worked, and what didn’t, gets logged for the next project.

Built to WCAG AAA, not just AA

Most video tools treat accessibility as a marketing word. VideoPM treats it as a contrast ratio you can check yourself, in either theme, on every screen.

  • Every piece of text meets a 7:1 contrast ratio, in both themes.
  • Every interactive element has a visible keyboard focus ring, not just a color change.
  • Every button, link, and form field has a 44×44px minimum touch target.
  • Light and dark themes are equally complete — try the toggle in the header.
